## Response workflow
1. **Preparation:** identify escalation paths and response authority.
2. **Detection and analysis:** validate alerts, preserve evidence, and determine scope.
3. **Containment:** select proportionate actions with appropriate approval.
4. **Eradication:** remove the confirmed cause after evidence is preserved.
5. **Recovery:** restore services and monitor for recurrence.
6. **Lessons learned:** document root cause, gaps, and preventive improvements.

## Current evidence status
No real phishing incident or malware-removal action is claimed in this module. Complete the case table using a clearly labeled synthetic scenario or an authorized exercise.

## Analyst discipline
Separate observed facts from hypotheses. Record timestamps with timezone, retain relevant logs, and do not claim compromise based only on a suspicious email or isolated alert.
